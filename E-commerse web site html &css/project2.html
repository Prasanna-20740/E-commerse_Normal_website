About Us

We are bunch of fragrance enthusiasts with a vision to redefine the Indian perfume landscape. We recognise the need for high-quality yet affordable fragrances that deliver great value to our customers. We want to build a fragrance brand that fulfils the aspirations of the young and modern India. Our goal is to make fragrances an integral part of people's daily lives.

At Perfume Lounge, we are driven by a set of core values that shape our brand identity:

INDIVIDUALITY: We believe in the power of uniqueness, our fragrances are crafted to complement individual personalities and styles.

SELF-EXPRESSION: Fragrance is language in itself. We empower individuals to express their authentic selves though our scents.

CREATIVITY: We embrace innovation and push the boundaries of fragrance creation to deliver exceptional olfactory experiences.

DIVERSITY: We celebrate the beauty of diversity and strive to create a fragrance collection that reflects the richness of human experience.
